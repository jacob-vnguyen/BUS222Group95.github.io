About Us

Red Maple Consulting is a student-founded consulting firm built on the principles of professionalism, integrity, and client focus. Formed by four University of Alberta students from both Edmonton and Calgary, the company represents the unity, balance, and shared purpose that define Alberta’s business landscape. The firm’s name — inspired by Canada’s iconic maple leaf — reflects its commitment to growth, strength, and meaningful contribution.


With combined expertise in business economics, law, accounting, and finance, we deliver practical, data-driven solutions that help organizations grow with confidence. Each member contributes unique strengths—from leadership and analysis to problem-solving and communication—ensuring every project benefits from diverse perspectives.


What sets Red Maple Consulting apart is its collaborative spirit. Each team member contributes not only their technical and academic knowledge but also their dedication to teamwork and communication. The firm values inclusivity, critical thinking, and a shared sense of accountability. Whether through detailed analysis, structured planning, or effective presentation, Red Maple Consulting strives to create outcomes that are clear, actionable, and impactful.
